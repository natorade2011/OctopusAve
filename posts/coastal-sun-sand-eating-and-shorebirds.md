There is nothing quite like a weekend getaway to reset, and our latest trip to Ocean Shores did exactly that. From backyard standoffs to incredible wildlife encounters, it was a weekend full of firsts and fantastic views.

## Standoffs in the Yard

We kicked things off just enjoying being down at the shore house. Rainy claimed her territory immediately, completely obsessed with having her own fenced yard to roam. She spent a good portion of the weekend locked in a minor, highly dramatic feud with a local backyard bunny. No harm done, but plenty of intense staring contests ensued!

![Rainy on patrol in the gravel yard with her ball](assets/rainy_patrol.jpg)

## Sadie’s First Beach Day (Featuring a Sand Buffet)

The absolute highlight of the trip was taking Sadie out for her very first beach day. The ocean air was perfect, but Sadie had her own agenda for the afternoon: attempting to eat as much sand as humanly possible. 

When Meaghan and I stepped in to shut down the sand buffet, she was not thrilled. Despite the brief, sandy protest, it was an unforgettable milestone.

![Meaghan smiling on the path, camera ready for beach milestones](assets/meaghan_path.jpg)

## Wildlife Action at Oyhut Bay

When we weren't managing toddler culinary experiments, we spent time exploring around Oyhut Bay, and the wildlife absolutely put on a show for us.

The water was teeming with **Brown Pelicans**. It was incredible watching them glide seamlessly just inches above the surf and then suddenly pivot to dive-bomb straight into the water after fish.

![The stunning shot of the Brown Pelican skimming the water](assets/pelican_skim.jpg)

As if the pelican airshow wasn't enough, we caught some high-altitude drama. A massive **Bald Eagle** made an appearance, only to immediately get completely bullied and chased off by a pair of fiercely territorial crows. You have to love the sheer confidence of a crow.

![The eagle being pursued across the blue sky](assets/eagle_bully.jpg)

It was the perfect blend of relaxing family time and incredible nature photography. Ocean Shores delivers every single time, and we're already counting down until we can head back down to the coast.
