## We are officially live!

Welcome to our online avenue! We are a couple living right along the shores of West Seattle, and we are so excited to share our daily slice of Pacific Northwest heaven with you. Named after the actual street we live on, **OctopusAve** is a lifestyle channel documenting our home design projects, beachside routines, and favorite local travel spots.

We are starting this blog in tandem with our brand new channels—make sure to follow **@OctopusAve** on TikTok and Instagram to see our daily video reels, room styling transitions, and local vlogs!

### What You'll Find Here

We want this blog to be an interactive companion to our videos, sharing detailed guides that don't fit into a short TikTok clip:

- **Coastal Modern Interior Design:** Step-by-step DIY guides, styling tips, and tours showing how we balance high-end coastal luxury with cozy, organic PNW elements.
- **West Seattle Travel Guides:** Hyper-local recommendations for the best coffee walks, beach trials, vinyl crates, and sunset views in our neighborhood.
- **Content Creator Journals:** Behind-the-scenes vlogs of what it's like to build a brand, film in a shore house, and connect with other ocean lovers.

We are so incredibly grateful to have you here at the very start of our journey. Grab a coffee, make yourself at home, and let's explore life by the water together!
