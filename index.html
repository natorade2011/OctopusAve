Our Story

Welcome to Our Sanctuary

Two creators, one coastal block, and endless Puget Sound sunsets.

We are a couple who fell in love with a quiet, beachside street in West Seattle. Living right on Octopus Ave, we wanted to capture the organic, oceanfront elegance of our Pacific Northwest shore house and the cozy beach community that surrounds it.

What started as simple weekend styling and local beach walks quickly grew into a passion. We decided to hit "record" on our daily life, starting @OctopusAve on TikTok and Instagram to build a community of design enthusiasts, travel lovers, and sunset watchers.

Whether you're here to find the perfect home decor tips, plan a perfect 48-hour Seattle getaway, or just escape with us into beautiful shore house vlogs, we're so glad you've landed on our avenue. Let's make every day feel like a weekend by the water.

Outdoor & Sunset

Alki Beach Boardwalk

📍 Alki Ave SW, Seattle, WA 98116

A beautiful 2.5-mile paved path stretching along the ocean with panoramic views of the Seattle skyline. Grab a coffee, rent some skates, or simply walk along the seawall like a local.

"Our absolute favorite spot for sunrise lavender lattes. In the evenings, you can watch seals popping up in the water!"
Shopping & Dining

The West Seattle Junction

📍 California Ave SW & SW Alaska St

The historic, bustling downtown core of the West Seattle peninsula. Full of local, independent businesses, records stores, cozy bakeries, bookstores, and incredible seafood restaurants.

"Spend your Sundays browsing through crates of classic vinyl at Easy Street Records, then head to the Farmers Market for fresh bakery goods!"
Nature & Hiking

Lincoln Park

📍 8011 Fauntleroy Way SW, Seattle, WA 98136

A spectacular 135-acre park situated on a headland. Offers miles of winding forest trails beneath giant Douglas firs, grassy picnic tables, rocky shores, and the Colman outdoor saltwater pool.

"It feels like you're deep inside an ancient rainforest, but the trail opens right up to a gorgeous beach looking out at Vashon Island."
